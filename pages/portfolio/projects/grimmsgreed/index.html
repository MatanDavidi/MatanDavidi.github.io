Grimm's Greed

Take it all... If you can carry it

Developed part-time in a group with 5 other students over the course of a semester as a practical assignment for the "Game Programming Laboratory" lecture during the second semester of my master's course at ETH Zürich, Grimm's Greed is a 2D 4-person multiplayer platform-shooter video game.
The objective of the game is to collect as much gold as possible and bring it to your base while preventing the opponents from doing the same.
